STEMonsters Interactives

Interactive pages for STEM concept building, Grades 3–12

These are self-contained, single-page interactives built by STEMonsters Education for use in our First Principles | Hands-on | Inquiry-led sessions. Each one lets students change something, watch what happens, and predict the next step before it appears.

How to use them

There is no public catalogue here. Your STEMonsters facilitator or teacher shares a direct link to the interactive for your session. Open it in any modern browser on a laptop, tablet or phone — nothing to install, and it works offline once loaded.

For teachers

  • Project the interactive and use the built-in prompts (for example, "predict the next row before pressing Halve Δt").
  • Keyboard shortcuts are listed at the bottom of each page for quick classroom control.
  • Links are stable — once shared, an interactive stays at the same address.
